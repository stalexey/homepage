Creating the Elemental Vortex in Avatar: Fire and Ash
  Abstract  ACM
Overview
The Flux Devil, an elemental vortex with a strong magnetic field in Avatar: Fire and Ash, is made up of plasma, water, and, in the climactic sequence, fire. This talk covers building the individual elements and combining them into a cohesive whole.
Authors
Nicholas Illingworth, Alexey Stomakhin, Murali Ramachari, James Robinson, John Edholm, Sam Cole
