Advancing Large-Scale Water Simulation in Avatar: Fire and Ash
  Abstract  ACM
Overview

Authors
Sean Flynn, Alexey Stomakhin, Joel Wretborn, Noh-hoon Lee, Job Guidos, Nicholas Illingworth, James Robinson, Ryan Bowden
